# JobPilot AI

An AI-powered career assistant that helps job seekers discover relevant opportunities, understand their suitability for roles, and generate tailored application materials.

Built as a rapid prototype for the Founder’s AI Developer application at Launchpad Build AI.

---

## Problem

Modern job searching is fragmented.

Candidates often spend significant time:

* Searching across multiple job boards
* Comparing job requirements manually
* Re-entering the same information repeatedly
* Tailoring cover letters for every application
* Determining whether a role is a good fit

This creates unnecessary friction and slows down the application process.

---

## Solution

JobPilot AI simplifies the process through a CV-first workflow.

Users upload their CV once and the platform:

1. Extracts skills and experience
2. Builds a candidate profile
3. Matches relevant opportunities
4. Calculates a job match score
5. Highlights strengths and skill gaps
6. Generates tailored cover letters

The goal is to reduce repetitive work and help candidates focus on applying for the right opportunities.
